FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Have questions about our programs, fees, or anything else? Find answers here.

Do I need any tech background to join?

No. You don't need a degree, a computer science certificate, or previous tech experience. Most of our graduates started from zero — including a medical doctor, a finance executive, and a former farm worker. You need computer literacy, teachability, willingness to put in the work — and to pass our intake assessment.

How long are the programmes?

The Software Engineering and AI (Full Stack AI Developer) and Product Design and AI programmes are 18 months. The AI Program (Talent Accelerator) is 16 weeks, with a 12-week accelerated track by assessment. The AI Advantage Programme runs over 3 Saturdays.

Can I join while working or still in school?

Yes — that's exactly what our weekend class is for. Both delivery modes (in-person and online) offer weekday or weekend schedules, so you can train without leaving your current job or studies.

I'm not in Accra. Can I still join?

Yes. Our online classes are live and interactive — same sessions, same instructors, same milestones as in-person. Students have joined from Kumasi, Cape Coast, Tamale, and from Nigeria, Cameroon, Liberia, and The Gambia.

Are you sure people get jobs after?

89% of our graduates are employed after the programme — a number that is independently audited, not a claim we made up. Our graduates work at Microsoft, Andela, mPharma, Hubtel, and companies in Germany, the UK, the US, and Canada.

Can I really start with zero deposit?

Yes. Through our Income Share Agreement with Chancen International, eligible students start with no deposit and pay tuition only after landing a job. Scholarships of up to 50% and payment plans are also available.

Does Codetrain provide laptops?

Not currently — you'll need your own laptop for training.

Software Engineering and AI (Full Stack AI Developer)

Graduates of this programme build and ship AI-powered software products — and compete for engineering roles at Ghanaian companies, African startups, and global employers in Germany, the UK, the US, and Canada. No prior experience required.

Curriculum

What you'll build, phase by phase

Three milestone-gated phases. You cannot advance without passing each milestone — so what you know at the end is real.

M 1–6
Phase 1 · Foundation

Full-stack fundamentals

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript fundamentals. Build your portfolio page and a JavaScript mini-app, then move into React and frontend development and a full CRUD application. AI tools are introduced from Month 3. Milestone 1: build and deploy a full MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) application.

M 7–12
Phase 2 · AI Integration

Building with AI

TypeScript. Working with LLM APIs (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini). Building RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines and AI agents. A team sprint project building a real product together. Milestone 2: build and deploy an AI-powered product.

M 13–18
Phase 3 · Production

Production AI systems

Multi-agent AI systems. Production monitoring and evaluation. System hardening. A capstone project — a real-world problem of your choosing, deployed to live users. Milestone 3: a production AI system with real users, evaluated by industry reviewers.

Milestones

Three milestones gate the programme

Milestone When What is assessed
M1 — Full MERN App End of Month 6 Functionality, code quality, code explanation, deployment, AI tool usage
M2 — AI-Powered Product End of Month 12 AI feature quality, architecture, code explanation, production quality, team collaboration
M3 — Production AI System End of Month 18 System design, AI capability, production hardening, code explanation, AI tool evidence
Outcomes

  • Build full-stack MERN applications and deploy them to live URLs
  • Work in engineering teams on real client projects
  • Compete for remote AI engineering roles internationally

Product Design

Graduates design digital products from research through to live, published applications — working in Ghana's growing tech sector, African startups, and with international clients who need African designers who understand local contexts.

Curriculum

What you'll build, phase by phase

Four milestone-gated phases — fundamentals first, then AI-assisted workflows, then real, published products.

M 1–3
Phase 1 · Design Foundations

Fundamentals before AI

Visual design principles. Typography, colour, layout. Research methods, wireframing, brand identity, and accessibility. No AI tools in this phase — you must understand design fundamentals first. Milestone 1: brand identity and app screens.

M 4–6
Phase 2 · AI-Assisted Design

Design faster and smarter with AI

AI tools introduced from Month 4. AI-assisted product design, component libraries, usability testing, developer handoff, and Figma mastery. Milestone 2: a full product design with AI workflow evidence.

M 7–9
Phase 3 · Building & Publishing

Ship real products with Framer

Framer — a no-code design tool for building real websites and apps. Motion design, collaboration with developers, and a real client brief. Milestone 3: a live, published Framer product — it must work on the assessment panel's own device.

M 10–18
Phase 4 · Strategy & Graduation

Strategy, portfolio & capstone

Design strategy and international design standards. Portfolio development. Technical writing — 3 published blog posts. A capstone project on a problem you choose, with at least 5 real users. Milestone 4: graduation, assessed by industry reviewers.

Milestones

Four milestones gate the programme

Milestone When The bar
M1 — Brand Identity + App Screens End of Month 3 Pass threshold on design fundamentals
M2 — Full Product Design + AI Workflow End of Month 6 Pass threshold, with AI workflow evidence
M3 — Published Framer Product End of Month 9 The site must be live on the panel's device on assessment day
M4 — Graduation Capstone End of Month 18 Minimum 5 real users — regardless of design quality
Outcomes

What graduates can do

  • Design full digital products from user research through to published, live application
  • Use Figma professionally for UI/UX design and developer handoff
  • Use Framer to build and publish real websites and apps without coding
  • Integrate AI tools into their design workflow purposefully and critically
  • Present and defend design decisions to clients and stakeholders
  • Write and publish technical content about their design process
  • Work with development teams on real products
Who it's for

This programme is right for you if…

  • You're a complete beginner with no design experience
  • You're a working professional in any field who wants to move into product design or UX
  • You're visual and creative and want a structured, professional pathway
  • You've used apps and wanted to design them instead
  • You work in finance, healthcare, education, admin, or communication and want to pivot into tech
